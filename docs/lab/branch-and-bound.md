# Branch-and-bound step by step

A two-variable problem solved by branch-and-bound: at each step a node, its relaxation and the two branches \(x_j \le \lfloor \tilde x_j \rfloor\) and \(x_j \ge \lceil \tilde x_j \rceil\), with the tree next to the plot. Choose the problem and the node selection rule; ◀ ▶ one step at a time, “Run” to see them all.
